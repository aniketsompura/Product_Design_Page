# SectionHeader

The opening of a page section: a frame label, a big Funnel Display title and a short lede in `ink-2`.

**Use it** at the top of every section and every Hub page that isn't a story.

**Markup:** `<header class="cv-section-head">` with `cv-frame-label`, an `<h2>` (`display-section`, balanced wrapping) and a `<p>` lede (max `text-max`). Leave `space-12` to `space-20` below it before the content. Use `cv-eyebrow` (“<b>01</b> The spark”) instead of the frame label when the section is a numbered step.

- Do: write the title as a plain claim in sentence case, ending with a full stop (“Six crafts, one team.”).
- Do: keep the lede to two sentences.
- Don't: center it; sections align to the page's left edge.
