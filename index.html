Our process, from spark to shipped

01 The spark

It starts with a spark.

Sometimes a product manager spots an opportunity on the roadmap. Sometimes we notice people struggling: a dip in the numbers, a frustrated support ticket, a workaround nobody should need. We pull those signals together until one problem is clearly worth solving.

  • User research
  • Analytics
  • Design audits
  • Opportunity framing

02 Brainstorm

Then we fill the whiteboard.

We frame the problem as a question and bring product, engineering and research into the room. Everyone writes ideas on sticky notes. We group them, debate them and vote until one direction clearly stands out.

  • Workshops
  • How might we
  • Affinity mapping
  • Dot voting

03 Wireframe

Structure before style.

The winning idea becomes a flow and a set of grey-box wireframes. Without color to distract us, we focus on hierarchy, layout and how someone gets from A to B. Then we test rough versions with real people while changes are still cheap.

  • User flows
  • Information architecture
  • Wireframes
  • Usability testing

04 High fidelity

Then the color comes in.

Wireframes become polished screens built from our design system: color, type, spacing, components and motion. We check every detail for accessibility and consistency, so the whole product looks and feels like one product.

  • Visual design
  • Design system
  • Prototyping
  • Accessibility

05 Build

We ship it with engineering.

We work beside the engineers who build it. We annotate specs, answer questions right in the file, map designs to code tokens and review builds until what ships matches what we designed.

  • Dev handoff
  • Design tokens
  • Design QA
  • Release

06 In people’s hands

And then it’s in people’s hands.

The real test is someone finding a receipt on a busy Tuesday. We watch how it gets used, listen to feedback and track the numbers. What we learn becomes the spark for the next improvement.

  • Product analytics
  • Feedback loops
  • Iteration
  • Outcomes

# What we do

Six crafts, one team.

Every project gets the mix it needs. Most of the time, that means several of us working on the same problem from different angles.

◇ Research

User research

Interviews, usability tests and data reviews that tell us what people need and where they get stuck.

Interview guides · Test reports · Insights

◇ Interaction

UX & interaction design

Flows, information architecture and the small moments in between: what happens on tap, on error and on an empty screen.

Journeys · Flows · Wireframes

◇ Visual

Visual & UI design

Color, type, layout and craft that make every screen clear, consistent and recognizably ours.

UI kits · Screens · Illustration

◇ Systems

Design systems

Reusable components and tokens, shared with engineering, so every team ships consistent and accessible UI faster.

Components · Tokens · Guidelines

◇ Prototyping

Prototyping & motion

Clickable prototypes and motion studies that let us feel an idea before anyone writes production code.

Prototypes · Motion specs · Demos

◇ Accessibility

Accessibility

Contrast, focus order, screen readers and plain language get checked in every review, not at the end.

Audits · Annotations · Checklists
